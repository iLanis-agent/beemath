BeeMath

Honest backyard bee math. Winter stores by climate, syrup by weight, mites by count, and harvest numbers that don't lie to you.

Winter is a number. 60 lb mild, 75 moderate, 90 cold - per hive, on the scale, before the cluster forms. The verdict says covered or short, and how many pounds short.
Syrup is weighed. Ratios are by weight, not cups: a gallon of water is 8.34 lb, so fall 2:1 means 16.7 lb of sugar per gallon of water. The classic cup-measuring mistake starves colonies quietly.
Mites are counted. An alcohol wash over ~300 bees, a percent, and the treatment line at 2-3%. "The hive looks strong" is not a measurement - colonies that skip the count die in November.
